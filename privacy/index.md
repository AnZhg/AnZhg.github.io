# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026._

This policy covers the Mac apps published by AnZhg. In short: **these apps do not
collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.** They are built to work entirely
on your own device.

## What stays on your device

Everything you make or set in an AnZhg app stays on your Mac — or in your own iCloud
Drive, if that is where you keep your files:

- **Your documents and content** are saved wherever you choose to save them. An app reads
  and writes only the files you open or save yourself.
- **Settings and per-document state** — preferences, and small pieces of state such as a
  window's size or a document's bookmarks — are stored locally, or alongside the document
  itself. If a document lives in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs that state together with the
  file across your own devices; it is never sent to the developer.

AnZhg apps have no accounts and no sign-in, show no advertising, and contain no analytics,
tracking, or crash-reporting code and no third-party software-development kits. Because
the developer never receives any of this information, there is nothing for the developer
to access, change, or delete on your behalf — you remain in full control of your files
and settings.

## Internet access

As a rule, AnZhg apps make no network connections at all: they neither send nor download
anything, and they have no online features, accounts, or downloadable content. Where a
particular app does something that uses the internet, it is always an action you start
yourself — for example, choosing a menu item that opens a web page in your browser — and
it is described in that app's section below. The app itself still sends nothing; your
browser, and whatever site you then choose to visit, take over from there.

## The Mac App Store and Apple

AnZhg apps are distributed through the Mac App Store. Your purchase, downloads, and any
usage statistics are handled by Apple under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer receives
only the aggregated, non-identifying reports that Apple provides to developers, such as
totals of sales and crashes. If you choose, at the system level, to share diagnostics
with developers, Apple may include crash reports in those aggregates; the apps do not
gather them themselves.

## Children's privacy

AnZhg apps are suitable for all ages, including children, and collect no personal
information from anyone. Where an app offers a way to contact the developer through a
third-party service (such as GitHub), that service may have its own age requirements,
noted in the app's section below; a younger user can ask an adult for help, or use a Mac
App Store review instead.

## Individual apps

### Nomogram

Nomogram is a Mac app for programming with flowcharts. It follows everything above, and
in particular:

- Your programs are saved as `.nomogram` files wherever you choose. Breakpoints and how
  each document's window was last left — its size, zoom, and scroll position — are stored
  alongside the document in the file's extended attributes, and sync through your own
  iCloud Drive if the file is kept there.
- Nomogram's **Help** menu offers **Report a Bug…** and **Report a Translation
  Problem…**. Choosing either opens a page in your web browser; the app itself sends
  nothing. (For a translation report, the app adds only the language it is currently
  showing, so the form can start in the right place.) If you then fill in and submit that
  form, your report is posted as a public issue on GitHub and is handled under
  [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
  Submitting currently requires a GitHub account, which GitHub does not allow for children
  under 13. Because the report is public, please do not include anything you would not
  want others to see. If you would rather not use GitHub, you can leave a review on the
  Mac App Store instead.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new
"last updated" date.

## Contact
